Research

Quantum field theory · noncommutative geometry

My research focuses on quantum field theory and noncommutative geometry — renormalisation on curved space–times, noncommutative gauge theories and matrix models.

Since joining ISTOM, I have also been working on statistics applied to biology and agronomy.
